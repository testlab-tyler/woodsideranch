## Type — Major Second (1.125) from 16px
display 48/350/1.1 · heading-lg 40/350/1.1 · heading 36/300/1 · heading-sm 32/300/1.5
subheading 24/350/1.2 · 20/300/1 · body 18/350/1.3 · body-sm 16/400/1.5
Weights 300/350/400/500. Letter-spacing -0.015 to -0.02em at 24px+.
Fallback named by the system: Inter (300,400,500) or General Sans. Mono: JetBrains Mono / IBM Plex Mono, +0.015em.

## Shape
Base unit 8px · max width 1200px · section gap 64px · card padding 16px · element gap 8px
Radius: inputs 8px · cards 16px · large cards 32px · buttons/badges/pills 1000px

## Hard rules
- NO shadows, NO elevation, NO gradients of any kind
- NO weight 600+ on headlines
- NO saturated color outside the five greens
- NO pure white
- NO square or 4-8px radii on buttons/badges - pills are non-negotiable
- Lime Pulse never fills a large area

## Deviations taken for Woodside Ranch (documented, deliberate)
1. Body copy set at 400 weight rather than 350. The system reserves 400-500 for
   body, so this is on-system - chosen because the audience skews older.
2. Mono is used for phase codes, document dates and file meta - the direct
   translation of Seed's product-code treatment (DS-01, DM-02).
