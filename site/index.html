Annual dues

One hundred dollars a year, for every phase.

Dues fund the association’s insurance, common areas, fire safety work and the cost of running the board. There are now two electronic options as well as payment by post.

When paying electronically, please put your Woodside Ranch address in the payment notes. The friends-and-family option is preferred, as it keeps the fees off the association.

Caldera Ranch

Full timeline and documents

A 716-unit development on 91.4 acres south of Knott Road, across from Caldera High School. It is the focus of the City of Bend’s one-time Urban Growth Boundary expansion, and it will affect Woodside Ranch.

Next 2 September 2026

Bend City Council public hearing to decide on changes to the Conceptual Plan.
