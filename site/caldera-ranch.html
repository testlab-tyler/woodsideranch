Caldera Ranch

Caldera Ranch is the 716-unit development which is the focus of the City of Bend Urban Growth Boundary expansion, conceptually approved by the City Council in December 2024. The site is south of Knott Road, on 91.4 acres, across from Caldera High School — and it will be impacting Woodside Ranch.
