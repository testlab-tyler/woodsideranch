Wildland–urban interface

If you only read one page on this site, read this one.

Woodside Ranch borders the Deschutes National Forest. These are the three things to do before a fire, and the two ways out during one.

Evacuation routes Sign up for alerts

Before a fire

Three things, none of which take an afternoon.

FS—01
Sign up for Deschutes Alerts

The county’s official emergency notification system. This is how an evacuation order reaches you.
FS—02
Install Watch Duty

Free, volunteer-run wildfire tracking. Faster than the news, and it covers Central Oregon well.
FS—03
Book a home hardening assessment

A walk-around of your property with someone who knows what catches. Free to Woodside Ranch owners.
