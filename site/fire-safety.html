Fire safety

Are you ready?

Wildfire is a common threat to all residents of Woodside Ranch and to everyone in Central Oregon. The best way to combat this threat is to be prepared and be informed.

Central Oregon Fire Info

The official publishing source for comprehensive fire, health and air quality information in Central Oregon. Current and planned prescribed burns, wildfires, and text alert sign-up.
Free app
Watch Duty

iOS and Android app giving a graphic display of fire locations, size and accompanying evacuation zones, run by a volunteer organisation that consolidates reports from multiple sources.

Notifications and alerts

Emergencies happen with little or no notice.

Deschutes Alerts uses reverse-911 technology to send emergency notifications about threats to your safety. You can opt in to receive them by phone call, text message and email.

Protect your investment

Home hardening assessments.

Would you like a second set of eyes on how prepared your home is to survive a wildfire?

Trained volunteer assessors within the neighborhood will arrange to walk your property with you and give you suggestions, tips and insights into things you can do to harden your home’s defenses.
