The association

A not-for-profit, operated by the residents of Woodside Ranch.

Woodside Ranch was developed in six phases, and each phase is governed by its own distinct set of CC&Rs. The association is a 501(c)(4) not-for-profit overseen by a Board of Directors elected by the membership at the Annual Membership Meeting. The board appoints officers and committees to manage day-to-day activities.

Board of directors — as of 31 May 2026

Five directors, two-year terms.

Each board member serves a two-year term and may also serve as an officer. The board can be reached at board@woodsideranch.net.

Standing committees

Six committees, all volunteers.

Each has its own address. Write to the committee, not the board.

ACC

Architectural Control

The CC&Rs define the ACC’s purpose as helping ensure and maintain the beauty of Woodside Ranch. Its powers vary by phase — in some phases pre-approval is required before exterior home or landscaping changes begin, in others the committee is advisory.

The association year

Three dates the association keeps.

Dates are posted on the calendar as the board and the Social Committee fix them.

Spring Annual Membership Meeting

The board is elected and the year’s budget is presented. Every owner may attend and vote. The 2026 meeting was held on 31 May.

Summer Summer Social

An evening for neighbours, organised by the Social Committee. It returned in July 2026 after an extended absence.

Winter Winter Warmer

The second of the two annual socials, and the association’s last gathering of the year.
